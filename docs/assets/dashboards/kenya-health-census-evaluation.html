All 12 Qwen cases were manually reviewed. No live facility inventory or health outcomes were evaluated.

Method

Text was extracted from the September 2023 Kenya Health Facility Census. TF-IDF keyword retrieval and sentence-embedding semantic retrieval ranked pages for 8 answerable and 4 out-of-scope questions. A local Qwen model received retrieved page text and was reviewed for factual accuracy, page support, denominator handling, and appropriate refusal.

Limitations: 12 questions, seed-page labels may omit valid pages, and only 12 questions were tested, and the seed-page labels may omit valid pages. The census is historical and aggregated. Qwen correctly answered 7 of 8 answerable cases in the reviewed set, with a denominator error on Q02. For the four out-of-scope cases, it generally abstained, but added unsupported claims in Q10. This prototype does not provide current facility stock or clinical advice.
