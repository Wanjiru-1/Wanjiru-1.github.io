# Projects

My research focuses on environmental change and access to opportunity. In Nakuru, I compared satellite observations from two April–June seasons to estimate how vegetation cover changed. In Nairobi, I mapped 20 reported flood-prone neighbourhoods. My labour force participation report compares eight African countries, while my maize forecasting research tests whether predictive models can improve on last month’s price in three Kenyan markets.

I use geospatial analysis and Earth observation to study where conditions change, business intelligence to make comparisons clear, and machine learning to test predictions against a baseline. My data engineering work connects the records behind those analyses, from membership registration to farm geodatabases. My goal is to give programme teams and local decision-makers evidence they can check, interpret and use. Personal Projects is where I explore other interests through apps such as Skin in the Game.
