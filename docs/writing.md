## Published articles

### NAKURU NDVI ANALYSIS (DROUGHT VS FLOOD YEAR)

*Published on Medium · October 2026*

How I used Sentinel-2 imagery, Google Earth Engine, and GIS to compare vegetation greenness in Nakuru County during April–June 2022 and 2024. The article explains the workflow, findings, and limitations of my final-year research, with a link to the interactive map and full report.
